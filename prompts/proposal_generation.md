# Proposal Generation Prompt (v1.0)

Generate a structured B2B project proposal for a qualified prospect.

## Sections Required:
1. Executive Summary
2. Problem Statement & Observed Bottlenecks
3. Proposed Solution Architecture
4. Scope of Deliverables & Features
5. Implementation Timeline (Phased)
6. Commercial Investment & Payment Terms
7. Key Assumptions & Prerequisites
8. Next Steps & Kickoff

All proposals require explicit owner approval prior to transmission.
