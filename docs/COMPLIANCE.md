# Jurisdictional Compliance & Suppression Policies

OWAIS OUTREACH AI is built on strict compliance with global B2B marketing regulations and ethical boundaries.

---

## Country Compliance Matrix

| Country | Relevant Law | Cold B2B Allowed? | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| **USA** | CAN-SPAM Act | Yes | Physical postal address, non-deceptive headers, clear opt-out. |
| **UK** | PECR & UK GDPR | Yes (Corporate) | Legitimate interest, company identity, instant opt-out. |
| **Canada** | CASL | Yes (Implied) | Conspicuously published email without negative notice, role-relevant message. |
| **Australia** | Spam Act 2003 | Restricted / Paused | Conspicuous publication or prior relationship. Manual verification required. |
| **New Zealand**| UEMA 2007 | Yes (Deemed) | Business capacity, role-relevant, functional unsubscribe. |
| **Singapore** | Spam Control Act | Yes | Sender identity, unsubscribe mechanism. |
| **UAE** | PDPL / TDRA | Yes | Commercial transparency, opt-out. |
| **EU** | GDPR / ePrivacy | Yes (B2B) | Legitimate interest assessment, role-relevance, easy opt-out. |

---

## Suppression System

Addresses are permanently suppressed upon:
- Inbound opt-out keywords ("stop", "unsubscribe", "remove me", "don't email")
- Hard bounce delivery reports
- Spam complaints
- Manual administrative entry
