# Campaign Management & Sequence Strategy

OWAIS OUTREACH AI executes targeted, multi-touch campaigns aligned with specific industry pain points and tailored AI solutions.

---

## 1. Campaign Structure

A campaign configures:
- **Target Country**: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Singapore, UAE
- **Target Industry**: Dental, Hotel, Real Estate, School, HVAC, Law, Accounting, etc.
- **Minimum Lead Score**: Threshold (default: 75+)
- **Primary Offer**: AI Receptionist, Lead Qualification, Workflow Automation, Custom Systems
- **Sending Mode**: `DRY_RUN=true` or `LIVE`
- **Follow-up Cadence**: Day 0 -> Day 4 -> Day 9 -> Day 16 -> Stop

---

## 2. Automated Sequence Rules

1. **Step 1 (Day 0)**: Initial introduction (60-150 words) focused on one grounded observation and low-friction preview CTA.
2. **Step 2 (Day 4)**: Brief follow-up asking if they saw the preview concept.
3. **Step 3 (Day 9)**: Value-oriented ROI insight (e.g. 2-3 after-hours bookings covering setup).
4. **Step 4 (Day 16)**: Respectful break-up note offering the main agency website for future reference.
5. **Instant Sequence Termination**: Any inbound reply, opt-out, bounce, or manual status change halts all scheduled follow-ups immediately.
